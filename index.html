How a round works

Learn it in a minute

The in-game tutorial walks you through it with a real table and real cards.

1

Get your five cards

Everyone is dealt a hand of five. Your goal is to end the round with the lowest hand value.

2

Discard and draw

On your turn, discard one card or a matching pair, then draw a new card to improve your hand.

3

Call your show

Think yours is the lowest hand? Call Show: if you are right you score zero, but a wrong call costs you a penalty. The highest score each round is knocked out, and the last player standing wins.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Is 5 Cards free?

Yes. 5 Cards is free to download and play. You earn coins by playing and from daily rewards, and you can also top up with optional in-app purchases if you like. You never need to buy anything to play.

Is it gambling?

No. 5 Cards is a party card game played for fun. There are no cash prizes and coins can never be turned into money, goods or services. Just bragging rights and good vibes.

Can I play with my friends?

Absolutely. Open a private room, share the room code and your friends can jump straight in. You can also add friends and invite them to a game.

Which devices does it run on?

5 Cards is built for Android phones. It is coming soon to Google Play.

How do I sign in?

Continue with Google or Facebook, or jump in as a guest. We never see your passwords.

How do I delete my account?

In the game go to Settings, Support, Delete Account. No game installed? Email us and we will take care of it. See the account deletion page.
