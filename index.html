How a round works

Learn it in a minute

The in-game tutorial walks you through it with a real table and real cards.

Get your five cards

Everyone is dealt a hand of five. Your goal is to end the round with the lowest hand value.

Discard and draw

On your turn, discard one card or a matching pair, then draw a new card to improve your hand.

Call your show

Think yours is the lowest hand? Call Show: if you are right you score zero, but a wrong call costs you a penalty. The highest score each round is knocked out, and the last player standing wins.
